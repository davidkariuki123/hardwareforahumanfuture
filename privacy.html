Privacy.

What we collect on this site, why we use it, and the choices you have.

Effective July 26, 2026

Who we are

Gather Platforms Inc., doing business as Relay, controls the personal information described here. This policy covers sendrelays.com, preorder transactions, and related customer communications. A separate product privacy notice will describe Relay device and service data before shipment.

Information we collect

  • Order and contact data: name, email, phone number, billing and shipping address, items, amount, tax, order status, and support communications.
  • Payment data: Stripe and its payment partners collect and process payment credentials. We receive transaction identifiers, payment status, card brand, and limited payment details, not your full card number.
  • Site data: IP address, device/browser information, pages and referring links, and basic security logs collected by our hosting and infrastructure providers.

How we use information

We use it to process and fulfill orders; send receipts, development, delay, shipping, safety, and service messages; provide support; prevent fraud and secure our systems; calculate and meet tax, accounting, legal, and compliance obligations; improve the site and preorder experience; and establish or defend legal claims.

Legal bases and sharing

Depending on where you live, we process information to perform our contract, comply with law, pursue legitimate interests such as fraud prevention and service improvement, and, when required, with consent. We share information only as needed with processors and partners such as Stripe, payment networks, hosting and infrastructure providers, shipping and fulfillment providers, professional advisers, tax authorities, and government bodies where legally required. We do not sell personal information for money or use preorder data for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Retention and security

We retain order and transaction records for as long as needed to fulfill the order and meet tax, accounting, warranty, fraud-prevention, and legal obligations. Other records are kept only as long as reasonably necessary. We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, but no system is completely secure.

Your choices and rights

You may unsubscribe from optional marketing using the link in those messages. Operational order emails cannot be disabled while your order is active. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a portable copy of information, and to appeal or complain to a regulator. We may verify your identity and may retain information where legally permitted or required. Email privacy@sendrelays.com to make a request.

Children, transfers, and changes

This site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information. We operate in the United States and may process information there and in other countries where providers operate, using required safeguards. We will post policy changes here and update the effective date; material changes affecting an active order will be communicated when required.
